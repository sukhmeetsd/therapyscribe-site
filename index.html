Why local matters

No BAA required for this tool

Cloud scribes process protected health information on their servers, which is why they need Business Associate Agreements. TherapyScribe processes nothing off your device — there's no business associate in the loop, which keeps your compliance posture dramatically simpler.

Encrypted at rest

Sessions, transcripts, and notes are AES-256 encrypted on disk, with the key in your macOS Keychain. Recordings are deleted automatically once your note is generated (you can turn this off).

Works offline

After a one-time model download, everything works with Wi-Fi off. Airplane mode is a feature, not a bug.

How it works

  1. Import your session recording (or record in the app).
  2. Your Mac transcribes it and drafts a SOAP or DAP note — on its own chip, in a few minutes.
  3. Review, edit, and paste into your EHR. Done.

Notes are drafts: you review and sign off on every note, exactly as you would with an intern's draft. Nothing goes into a record without your eyes on it.

Don't take our word for it — verify it. Open Activity Monitor's Network tab (or Little Snitch) and transcribe a session. You will see zero network traffic from TherapyScribe. The only connections this app ever makes are the one-time model download and a license check that sends your license key and nothing else.
